Privacy policy

Clip Uploader · Effective November 18, 2026

Clip Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Fifty Fifty YouTube channel to upload that person's own clips. This page explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

What it accesses

  • Your YouTube account, through YouTube API Services, only after you sign in with Google and allow it. It uses that access to upload videos to your channel, set their title, description and publish time, change a video's privacy (for example, to cancel a scheduled post), and read the status of videos it uploaded and the name of the channel they go to.
  • Your Instagram account, only if you connect it, to publish Reels to that account and read the account's name.

It does not read your email, contacts, comments, watch history or any other account data.

Where your data is kept

The sign-in tokens that Google (and Meta, if connected) issue are saved only on the computer that runs Clip Uploader, in files that only that computer's user can read. The videos it uploads come from that same computer. Nothing is stored on any server run by Clip Uploader; it has none.

Sharing

Clip Uploader does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, does not show ads, and has no analytics or tracking. Data goes only to YouTube (and Instagram, if connected) to do the uploads you asked for. It is not used to train AI models.

Clip Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google and YouTube

Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove Clip Uploader's access at any time on your Google Account's third-party connections page (also reachable from security settings). Once access is removed, the saved tokens stop working; deleting them from the computer removes the last copy. To ask anything about your data, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
